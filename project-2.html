// Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing Lab

Piezoelectric Chiseling Tool

I had the opportunity to work on a design project with Professor Ping Guo and Malachi Landis in Northwestern's Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing Lab. I designed a chiseling tool for in-lab precision manufacturing.

Solidworks FEA Frequency Analysis Static Analysis

Objective

Design an ultra-precise chiseling tool, with in situ force, and displacement sensors. The goal was to design a tool with dual axis head displacement of 10 microns in each direction and a stiffness of 50 N/micron into the cut with a resonant frequency above working frequency of 5 kHz. The corresponding resonant modes would also follow the cutting motion of the chiseling tool.

Approach

To create the tool I designed and modeled a compliant mechanism in SolidWorks. Optimized compliance accuracy using frequency analysis and static analysis. Incorporated piezo strain gauges and load cells for force and displacement measurement.

Outcome

The results were a piezoelectric chiseling device operable up to 10 kHz with a head stiffness of 83 N/micron into the cutting direction.
