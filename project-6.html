Gait Glider

A collaboration with the Motion Analysis Lab at Shriners Children's Hospital to build equipment supporting clinical gait analysis.

Rapid Prototyping Bridgeport Mill Drill Press

Objective

Create a device to capture stable sagittal-plane camera footage of a patient walking across a 20 ft span for gait analysis.

Approach

Designed a frame mounted on tank-style wheels, using a neoprene rubber track around caster wheels for smooth rolling, fabricated a foldable handle hinge on a Bridgeport mill, and built sheet-metal track guards.

Outcome

Delivered a device that rolls smoothly over the gaps between floor tiles, keeping camera footage stable, with a foldable handle for storage — reducing total setup time by 30 seconds.
