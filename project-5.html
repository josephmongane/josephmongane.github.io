// Shirley Ryan AbilityLab

Scissor Seat

A collaborative design project with the Shirley Ryan AbilityLab to build a caregiver-assist device for fall recovery.

Rapid Prototyping Vertical Bandsaw Drill Press

Objective

Design a lightweight, portable tool that minimizes the physical strength a caregiver needs to lift a person with limited mobility off the ground after a fall, while remaining easy to use.

Approach

The team built a device that uses a scissor jack to lift a foldable seat, with the seat manufactured from acrylic plates cut on a vertical bandsaw.

Outcome

Created a collapsible, easy-to-use fall recovery tool delivering a 30:1 mechanical advantage, capable of lifting over 200 lbs.
